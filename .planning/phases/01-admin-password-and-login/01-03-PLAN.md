## Planning Decisions (Claude's discretion and refinements, recorded)

- **Delay as 429 + Retry-After, not a sleep:** a blocked attempt is refused before scrypt, so parallel requests cannot
  each burn ~0.4 s of CPU and 64 MiB after waiting (D-07 allowed either).
- **Curve:** wait `min(2^(n-1), 30)` seconds after the n-th consecutive failure; a failure more than 10 minutes after the
  previous one restarts the count; success deletes the entry; an address with an attempt in flight gets Retry-After 1.
- **Pruning:** at most once per 60 s of clock time, when the map holds more than 100 entries, drop entries that are not
  in flight, no longer blocked and older than 10 minutes; a hard cap of 10 000 entries evicts the oldest (Map insertion
  order); addresses are cut to 64 characters.
- **D-17 refinement (deliberate, a superset of what D-17 refuses):** check `Sec-Fetch-Site` first (accept `same-origin`
  and `none`), fall back to `Origin` only when it is absent (`null` never allowed), and accept when neither header exists
  (curl, old browsers). It survives a proxy that rewrites Host, and follows OWASP's current order.
- **No global limit (D-08):** the limit is per address only. Residual risk recorded in the threat model.

## Flagged Assumptions (edge probe: unclassified, surfaced, not silently dropped)

- **AUTH-03 (unclassified):** "slowed down" is assumed to be the 429 + Retry-After curve above, and "the same address" the
  last X-Forwarded-For entry (D-09), which a client without a proxy in front can forge, an accepted risk (D-09).
